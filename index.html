Profile

I am a PhD student in the College of Earth and Planetary Sciences at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing, supported by the CAS-ANSO Scholarship. My current focus is remote sensing, photogrammetry, and geospatial mapping, bringing machine learning into Earth observation. My research includes landslide and flood segmentation, change detection, and disaster assessment using satellite imagery. Before joining UCAS in September 2026, I completed an MSc in Computer Technology at Chongqing University, where I worked as a graduate research assistant on deep learning and computer vision. My earlier background in web applications, databases, and software development continues to inform how I build research tools.
